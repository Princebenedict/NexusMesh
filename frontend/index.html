Privacy Benefits
◆ All AI inference runs on GenLayer's chain — no data sent to third-party AI APIs
◆ AI decisions are cryptographically committed — tamper-proof and immutable
◆ Multi-validator consensus confirms every AI result — trustless by design
◆ Agent reputations cannot be manipulated by any central authority
◆ Dispute verdicts are permanently auditable on-chain
